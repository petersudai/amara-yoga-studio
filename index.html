Our Philosophy

Movement is how the body remembers it's safe

Amara began as a single sunrise class on a rented rooftop. Ten years on, the practice hasn't changed — we still start with breath, move at the pace of the body, and end in stillness. No mirrors, no performance. Just the work of arriving.

  • Small classes, capped at 14, so every alignment cue is personal
  • Daylight studio built around natural light and silence
  • Teachers trained in anatomy first, sequencing second
